# Using AI to decide a vote: Knesset 2026

One voter's experiment for Israel's 27 October 2026 election. A voice note on what he cares about became a 15-criterion rubric. Claude research agents gathered sourced evidence on all 37 lists on the ballot. One scoring agent graded every list, and the page ranks them. You can move the weights and watch the recommendation change.

The scores are AI judgements against one person's stated positions, not neutral ratings of the parties.

Source, evidence and PDFs: https://github.com/danielrosehill/Israel-Election-2026-Vote-Decision
