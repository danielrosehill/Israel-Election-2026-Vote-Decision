My long-standing critique of Israeli politics is that the whole litany of things I feel is broken and wrong with it stems from one rotten root: in the way this country is set up, in our electoral system here, there is no accountability baked into the system. This is my external critique, as someone who moved here from Ireland: Israelis are at once very worldly and, in a peculiar way (I'm having a hard time with some words today), insular, in the sense that this is their frame of reference and this is how politics are done here.

So naturally I've been reading about who's running in this election, and trying even to summon up the motivation to do that, because it's exhausting. And the reason I feel so profoundly disillusioned about the political situation is this. I could say, okay, hopefully Netanyahu won't be elected. He's the one person I'm definitely not voting for.
