# Scoring rubric

Derived from the voice note of 2026-10-26 (`context/voice-notes/2026-10-26-politics/`). Timestamps
(`mm:ss`) refer to the **original** recording's timeline, as in `transcript-timed` / the raw
transcript; the tidied audio is ~7 minutes shorter. Machine-readable copy: `rubric.json`.

Daniel's ask (40:26–41:30 in the original): define an objective rubric from the issues he
prioritises and where he stands on them, score every list running on 27 October 2026, then rank on
**Stream A** alone and on **A + B** combined.

- **Stream A** — everyday pain points: rent, housing instability, cost of living, bureaucracy, and
  Israel's global reputation ("a meta issue … everyone bears that in some way").
- **Stream B** — the divisive national issues: security, national identity, Haredi enlistment,
  judicial reform and the rest. Put "to one side" for the first ranking, not ignored.
- **Meta guide** (40:26–42:08): the party he wants says "this is our programme, it's consultative,
  we're here to serve you", cards on the table. A party defined by what it is against (or by its
  leader's grievance) scores low. Bar: a published policy PDF, ideally in all commonly spoken
  languages.

Each criterion is scored **0–5** against the anchors below. **n/e** (no evidence) is allowed and
is scored 0 for A1–A6 (a party with no stated policy on Daniel's top issues has, for his purposes,
no policy) and excluded from the denominator for Stream B.

## Scores and ranking

- **A score** = Σ(weight × score/5) over A1–A6, 0–100.
- **B score** = mean of the scored B items × 20, 0–100 (n/e excluded; at least 4 items needed, else
  "insufficient").
- **Combined** = 0.7 × A + 0.3 × B. The 70/30 split is an inference: the note puts Stream A first
  and calls the B issues important but over-weighted in Israeli politics. Rankings are also
  shown at 50/50 as a sensitivity check.
- **Disqualifier:** a list running Netanyahu as its PM candidate, or pledged to recommend only him
  (B3 ≤ 1), is flagged "excluded by stated choice", ranked but not recommendable.
- Lists with fewer than four scored Stream B items get no combined score; they are ordered by
  0.7 × A among themselves.
- **Viability:** the threshold is 3.25%. The note criticises splitter parties that split the vote
  (28:18). The recommendation table therefore shows each list's polled seats (mean of polls
  released 10–22 Oct 2026, from `Israeli-Elections-2026/data/polls/`) next to its score, and
  recommends among lists that are polling above the threshold, naming a best match below it
  separately.
