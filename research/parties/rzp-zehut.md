# Religious Zionist Party – Zehut (הציונות הדתית בראשות בצלאל סמוטריץ' וזהות בראשות משה פייגלין, ballot letters ט)
Leader: Bezalel Smotrich (No. 2 Feiglin; 3 Strock; 4 Rothman) | Components: Religious Zionist Party (registered as National Union–Tkuma); Zehut; Atid Ehad | Polled seats (10–22 Oct mean): 6.4 | Researched: 2026-10-27 by Claude Sonnet 5.5 (second pass: WebSearch EN+HE, WebFetch, curl)

## Platform availability (A6)
- RZP has a platform page: https://zionutdatit.org.il/מצע-המפלגה/ (confirmed fetched). Hebrew, web page linking one PDF per topic: diaspora/aliyah, Jewish identity, justice, infiltrators, family law, reservists, settlement/sovereignty, education, terror, economy, agricultural crime, nature, welfare, plus a mental-health plan uploaded Oct 2026 [1]. Most PDFs are uploads dated Dec 2021 / Oct 2022 (carried over from the 2022 campaign); the economy PDF is a single image-only page (2021 upload; headings seen: public sector, state budget, taxation, health, education, business environment; text not legible at the resolution I viewed) [1][2]. Housing, rent, cost of living, imports and bureaucracy are not covered on the page [1]. IDI links this page as RZP's platform; Zehut has no entry [3]. Not costed; Hebrew only (no other language seen); no consultative mechanism or electoral reform found. Joint 2026 platform with Zehut: none found. ## A1 Tenant protection
- No evidence found (searched EN + HE; platform page silent on rent). Zehut's 2019 approach is deregulation, not tenant protection (inferred) [4].
## A2 Housing affordability
- Smotrich (Finance Minister Dec 2022–2026 [5]) forecast a "substantial fall" in home prices and cited cheap periphery land tenders [6]; he demanded 50% of the discounted-housing programme for reservists while Goldknopf (UTJ) opposed, threatening to block renewal [7]. Smotrich said housing, rent and food are the core of the cost-of-living problem and had not been addressed in three years [8]. His office says 51,370 West Bank housing units approved since 2022; Peace Now counts 27,941 advanced in 2025 [6]. Zehut (2019): dismantle the Israel Land Authority, sell vacant land, fewer permits, land lottery [4]. Apartment prices were reported down 1.2% y/y mid-2026 (Likud site, self-reported) [9].
## A3 Cost of living and competition
- Personal-import VAT exemption (confirmed): Smotrich signed an order raising the limit from $75 to $150 (Dec 2025); the Knesset voted 59–25 to cancel it, Likud rebels voting against; he signed a $130 order the same day/night (Kan, Walla); the Knesset revoked that 59–23, restoring $75 (ToI) and he said it was the second cancellation [8][10][11][12]. Sources conflict on the limit now in force (Bhol: $75; Kan/Calcalist: $130) and on Bhol's date [12]. Chamber of Commerce and small businesses opposed it as discriminating against local retail [12][8].
- 2026 budget: promised to open banking and tax excess bank profits, income-tax cuts [13]; Likud minister Karhi: ministry "out of touch and elitist" [14]. Smotrich opposed Goldknopf's VAT-17% cut (NIS 8.5bn/yr) [8]. Zehut 2019: remove import barriers, low flat tax, end farm quotas [4]. Hebrew search for the AliExpress item confirmed the above.
## A4 Working state
- RZP platform sections on welfare, health and a mental-health plan (Oct 2026) exist but I could not read text [1]. Smotrich's budget NIS 800m to Haredi institutions [15]. No evidence on bureaucracy beyond Zehut's "crazy bureaucracy" line (2019, Breitbart) [4].
## A5 Global reputation
- Smotrich (Sep 2026): annex Gaza to the yellow line and Lebanon to the Litani; "go to war" in the West Bank [16]; proposed annexing 82% of the West Bank, UAE called it a red line (Sep 2025) [17]. No repair plan; platform has a diaspora/aliyah section [1].
## B1 Haredi enlistment
- Smotrich declined to state RZP's position on the Bismuth bill (Jan 2026); Solomon, Woldiger and Sofer opposed; Sofer said he would vote against [18]. Platform has a reservists programme [1].
## B2 Judicial overhaul
- RZP platform has a justice section [1]. Coalition passed Judicial Selection law 27 Mar 2025 [19]; Rothman (RZP No. 4) chaired the Constitution Committee that advanced the overhaul (background, not re-sourced in this pass).
## B3 Netanyahu
- Coalition partner; Netanyahu welcomed the RZP–Zehut union [20].
## B4 Far right in government
- Smotrich is himself a far-right minister; list advocates annexing the West Bank and Gaza [20]. Resigned in protest at Ben-Gvir's return demands (search snippet, undated) [6].
## B5 Palestinian question
- Opposes a state; "Colonisation 2030" (Feb 2026): nullify Oslo, sovereignty, encourage emigration from Gaza and Judea/Samaria [16]. Feiglin: annex Gaza and empty it of population [20].
## B6 Security and alliances
- Platform: terror, military/reservists, settlement/sovereignty sections [1].
## B7 Religion and state
- Platform: Jewish identity, family law, Jewish education [1].
## B8 October 7 accountability
- No evidence found (searched EN; platform justice section unread).
## B9 Diaspora / hasbara
- Platform has "Diaspora Jewry and aliyah" section [1]; no spending stance read.

## How the party presents itself
RZP: ideological (settlement, sovereignty, Jewish identity) with a topical PDF-per-subject platform page; Zehut: libertarian economics plus nationalism. Merged to clear the threshold; each can split post-election [20].
