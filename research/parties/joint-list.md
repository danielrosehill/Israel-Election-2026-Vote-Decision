# Joint List (הרשימה המשותפת, ballot letters ודם)
Leader: Yousef Jabareen (Hadash), then Ahmad Tibi (Ta'al) | Components: Hadash (Maki), Ta'al, Balad | Polled seats (10–22 Oct mean): 8.1 | Researched: 2026-10-27 by Claude Sonnet 5.5

## Platform availability (A6)
- **Confirmed:** published economic-social programme, web page, https://jointlist.org.il/he/economic-plan/ [1]. Hebrew and Arabic text; site nav links English and Russian versions (nav seen, English/Russian content not checked). Undated. Mostly uncosted (only illustrative figures). No consultative mechanism or electoral-reform proposal found on the page. Only the economic-social plan was read; no separate diplomatic/security platform found (searched: IDI party page lists only this URL [2]).

## A1 Tenant protection
- Confirmed: cap rent at 20% of household net income [1]. Nothing on lease terms, eviction, agent fees.
## A2 Housing affordability
- Confirmed: guaranteed public housing (rent or buy) for everyone homeless; national mortgage bank; interest-free mortgages for first-time buyers ("דירה ראשונה בלי ריבית"); mass construction via government companies; land to young couples; reinstate public housing law [1]. Also demands end to demolitions in Arab towns, planning/permits, recognition of Negev unrecognised villages [3].
## A3 Cost of living and competition
- Confirmed: minimum wage at 70% of average (~56 NIS/h); 100% COLA from first percent; VAT to 10%; income-tax exemption for low earners; tariffs to protect domestic production from dumped imports, incl. on US goods [1]. Protectionist leaning (relevant to rubric A3).
## A4 Working state
- Confirmed: free education from age 0; health-care tax revival for long-term care insurance; restore National Insurance benefits; reverse privatisation, repeal arrangements law; crime plan with timetable and funding for Arab towns [1][3]. Bureaucracy not addressed.
## A5 Global reputation
- No evidence found (searched: jointlist.org.il programme, news search).
## B1 Haredi enlistment
- No evidence found (searched: programme page; no stance found. Inferred: opposes military budgets/occupation, likely opposes criminal sanctions; not confirmed).
## B2 Judicial overhaul
- Inferred only (opposition bloc, calls to fight racism and supremacy [3]); no explicit statement found.
## B3 Netanyahu
- Confirmed: offers only confidence-and-supply to an alternative government that replaces Netanyahu, "no blank check", price is a "real safety net" incl. end to demolitions [3][4].
## B4 Far right in government
- Confirmed (framing): vows to fight "racism, supremacy" [3]. Inferred: strongly opposed.
## B5 Palestinian question
- Confirmed: programme calls for ending the occupation and settlement funding, deep military cuts [1]. Two-state wording not captured in sources read (inferred pro-Palestinian-statehood; Balad component historically differs).
## B6 Security and alliances
- Confirmed: deep cuts to military spending, end funding for Gaza war [1]. No alliances policy found.
## B7 Religion and state
- No evidence found (searched: programme page; only equal education funding across sectors [1]).
## B8 October 7 accountability
- No evidence found (searched: programme page, news).
## B9 Diaspora / hasbara spending
- No evidence found (searched: programme page).

## How the party presents itself
Programme-led on social-economic and Arab-equality issues (published plan, Hebrew/Arabic), with a conditional, anti-Netanyahu external-support stance; it also presents as a unity vehicle for the Arab public. Lists: CEC barred it 24 Sep 2026, Supreme Court reversed 2 Oct; Abu Shehadeh resigned from the list (data/parties.csv).
