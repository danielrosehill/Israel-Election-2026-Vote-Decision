# Yashar (ישר! עם איזנקוט לראשות הממשלה מאחדים את ישראל, ballot letters דרך)
Leader: Gadi Eisenkot | Components: Yashar; Yesodot Yisrael (CEC filing) | Polled seats (10–22 Oct mean): 21.4 | Researched: 2026-10-27 by Claude Sonnet 5.5

## Platform availability (A6)
- Published: **yes, short**. "10 Steps" brochure PDF (https://yasharwitheisenkot.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Yashar_10_Steps_Brochure.pdf, June 2026) plus web page /agenda_point/ with the 10-point agenda [1]. Site languages: Hebrew, English (/en/), Russian (/ru/) [1]. No Arabic or Amharic seen (confirmed from site nav only).
- Also a youth/young-adults package published 28 Sep 2026 (JPost) [2].
- Costed: no costing seen. Consultative mechanism: none found. Electoral-reform/accountability: PM term limit (point 7) [3]. No constituency reform found.
- Not fetched: the PDF itself (page summaries only).

## A1 Tenant protection
- Youth package (28 Sep 2026) includes "assistance and protection mechanisms for renters" ("מנגנוני סיוע והגנה לשוכרים"). No detail: no mention of eviction limits, tenancy at will, agent fees or enforcement. Confirmed (JPost) [2].
- The 10-points page has no rent policy (confirmed by page read) [1].
- Inferred/record: Roy Folkman, author of the 2017 Fair Rental Law (Kulanu; base bill, with Stav Shaffir), is listed among later additions to Yashar's list per Wikipedia [3][4]. That law set landlord-pays-broker-fee, deposit cap (one third of rent, max 3 months), repair duty, 90-day landlord / 60-day tenant notice; it dropped rent-rise limits [4][5]. Evidence of a candidate's record, not party policy. Wikipedia's wording on Folkman is unverified against a primary list.

## A2 Housing affordability
- Only housing item on the agenda page: "הטבות דיור" (housing benefits) steered to those carrying the security and economic burden (service/reserve) [1]. Confirmed.
- No supply plan found. 10th point: strong national planning authority aimed at 2048 [3].

## A3 Cost of living and competition
- Point 5: "תכנית לאומית למלחמה ביוקר המחיה": break up concentrations of economic power, strengthen competition, adopt international standards, invest in periphery infrastructure/transport/education/health [1]. Confirmed. No numeric targets, no import-liberalisation specifics found.
- Candidate Shaul Meridor (7th) says priorities include lowering cost of living [2].

## A4 Working state
- Periphery investment in transport, education, health [1]; "professional civil service" (point 7) [3]. Candidate priority "service and state education for all", rehabilitating north and south [2]. No digital-government or bureaucracy-specific plan found.

## A5 Global reputation
- Point 2: security strategy including "expands peace agreements" [3]; Jewish-and-democratic framing. No explicit reputation/diplomacy repair plan found.

## B1 Haredi enlistment
- Point 3: bring Haredi and Arab Israelis into military service, equal burden; coalition condition (July 2026): universal national service [3]. Confirmed.

## B2 Judicial overhaul
- Point 7: protect rule of law, judicial and legislative independence, minority and human rights [3]. Confirmed in general terms; no specific pledge to repeal 2023–26 laws seen.

## B3 Netanyahu
- Rules out serving under Netanyahu even in a rotation (JNS) [6]. Seeks to replace him as PM candidate [2]. PM term limit on agenda [3].

## B4 Far right in government
- No direct statement found (searched: Eisenkot Ben-Gvir Smotrich). Inferred: opposition bloc, rules out Netanyahu-led coalition [6].

## B5 Palestinian question
- Point 2: "expand peace agreements", preserve Jewish majority [3]. No statehood position found (searched twice).

## B6 Security and alliances
- Point 2: systemic security strategy, strengthen IDF, secure borders, expand peace agreements [3]. Youth package: +15% pay for conscripts, +50% for third-year combat soldiers; reserve duty cap 50 days/yr, 150 per 3 years [2].

## B7 Religion and state
- Point 8: "inclusive Judaism", Jewish and democratic state, more local-authority independence [3]. Courts Shas voters; would partner with Shas if it accepts his principles [3]. No civil marriage or Shabbat transport position found.

## B8 October 7 accountability
- Point 1: state commission of inquiry [3]. Four lists promise one: Yashar, B'Yachad, Yisrael Beytenu, Democrats (per one outlet) [7].

## B9 Diaspora / hasbara spending
- Point 9: aliyah goal of 1 million in 10 years; keeps Law of Return including Grandchild Clause [3]. No hasbara-spending position found.

## How the party presents itself
Leader-centred but programme-bearing: a 10-point "unite Israel" agenda, with the PM candidate's name in the list title; a centrist, security-credentialed alternative to Netanyahu. Housing and cost of living are one point of ten; rent is a single phrase.
