## A1 Tenant protection
- Searched EN + HE: no Shas policy. Shas held Interior (Moshe Arbel, Apr 2023–Jul 2025) and Health (Buso, Oct 2023–Jul 2025) [3]; neither covers rent law. Housing was UTJ's, not Shas's [3].
## A2 Housing affordability
- No Shas housing plan. Shas ministers did not hold Housing [3]. Deri's coalition deal included rotating into Finance with Smotrich (never executed per the table) [3].
## A3 Cost of living and competition
- Interior Ministry (Shas minister Arbel until Jul 2025) issued the 2026 arnona-discount regulations: eligibility rises from about 740k to 840k households, a 3-person household on NIS 6,500 moves from 60% to 90% discount; local authorities lose about NIS 1.1bn (Walla/Globes; Finance Ministry opposed) [4]. Attribution of the reform to Arbel specifically is inferred from the portfolio dates; regulations may have been issued after Levin took over (not verified). Shas MKs backed the NIS 800m Haredi allocations in the 2026 budget [5].
## A4 Working state
- Health: Buso (Shas) was minister Oct 2023–Jul 2025, resigned over the draft crisis [3][6]; held emergency-readiness reviews; no urgent-care policy found in EN search [6]. Interior/Arnona: see A3. Shas ministers quit government in 2026 over the draft; Deri stayed an observer in the Security Cabinet [5].

## B1 Haredi enlistment
- Deri: Shas will not join the next government without a law enshrining exemptions, like UTJ [7]. Shas's 11 MKs supported the Bismuth bill (full-time yeshiva students keep exemptions) while withholding budget votes until its process ended (ToI, Jan 2026) [8]. Shas ministers quit in 2026; MKs stayed in the coalition [5]. Campaign video on "family and IDF service" [9].
## B2 Judicial overhaul
