# Together (B'Yachad) (ביחד בראשות נפתלי בנט, ballot letters רק)
Leader: Naftali Bennett | Components: Bennett 2026; Yesh Atid (Lapid) | Polled seats (10–22 Oct mean): 11.8 | Researched: 2026-10-27 by Claude Sonnet 5.5
Note: this is the Bennett-Lapid list, not "Together We Will Succeed" (Avi Shaked, ballot letters בד), a different list in parties.csv.

## Platform availability (A6)
- Published: **yes, web-only, no PDF**. https://be-yahad.org.il/plans/ ("ביחד נתקן – תכניות"): about 25 plan pages (housing, cost of living, transport, health, education, small businesses, religion and state, national security, AI, Diaspora, etc.) [1]. Languages: Hebrew, English, Russian, Amharic [1]. No PDF links found.
- Costed: partly (targets such as food prices -30%; not budgets). Consultative: none found. Accountability: PM 8-year term limit, state commission on Oct 7, constitution proposal [2].
- Wikipedia cites JVL saying no comprehensive formal platform at formation; the plan pages appear to supersede that [2].

## A1 Tenant protection
- Housing plan page (be-yahad.org.il/plans/housing/) says nothing on evictions, agent fees or tenant protections (confirmed by page read) [3].
- Long-term rental supply: 100,000 long-term rental units in 10 years; 40% in a subsidised, regulated track at 30% below market; tax benefits and safety net for developers committing to 10+ year rental; tax benefits for private landlords on leases of 5+ years; "long-term certainty for tenants and landlords" [3]. Confirmed. This is supply and lease-length incentive, not tenant-rights law.
- Record: Lapid and Yael German (Yesh Atid) co-sponsored the 2017 Fair Rental Law (agent fee on landlord, deposit cap, repair duty, 90/60-day notice; no rent limits) [4][5]. Lapid's 2014 bill (with Livni) would have limited rent increases and required habitable dwellings; fell with Knesset dissolution (search summary of Bizportal; not fetched) [6]. Lapid, 19 May 2026: expanded state long-term rental programme with right to buy after 10 years [2].
- Bennett personal record on tenant law: none found.

## A2 Housing affordability
- Rent-to-own style: lease-purchase after ten years (Lapid, May 2026) [2]. NIS 1m home benefit for active reservists, priority in land tenders [3]. Urban renewal cut from 10+ years to about 5; targets 18 months local approval, 3 months permits, 24 months completion [3]. Confirmed. No curbs on investment-only stock found.
- Service-first benefits ("Meshartim law"): priority in housing benefits, arnona/electricity/water discounts for servers [1].

## A3 Cost of living and competition
- Cost-of-living page: cut supermarket-basket/food prices 30% in 3 years; lower import barriers; break up monopolies (limit large importers to one monopolistic brand); single food authority; gradual tariff reduction with direct farmer support; recognise international kosher certification; monopoly transparency; target OECD-average basket in 3 years [7]. Confirmed (party's own claims).
- Bennett, 12 Feb 2026: break monopolies and cartels, cut regulation; detailed economic plan 1 Jul 2026 [2]. Strongest A3 evidence among my four lists.

## A4 Working state
- Plans exist on transport, health, education, small businesses, public service [1]. Not read in detail.

## A5 Global reputation
- Platform goal: end regional isolation through regional alliances [2]. Bennett's "first Bennett government achievements" page [1]. No explicit reputation-repair plan read.

## B1 Haredi enlistment
- Both support mandatory conscription; end state funding for those who don't serve, work or use the state education system; "Meshartim law"; commission into "sabotage of Haredi enlistment" (Bennett, 30 Mar 2026) [1][2]. Confirmed.

## B2 Judicial overhaul
- Regulate Basic Laws and judiciary "through broad consensus"; constitution in the spirit of the Declaration of Independence (12 May 2026); 8-year PM term limit [2].

## B3 Netanyahu
- Core purpose is replacing Netanyahu; first act would be a state commission on Oct 7; term limit [2]. Bennett was PM 2021–22 after toppling the Netanyahu government [2].

## B4 Far right in government
- No direct statement read (searched: Together Ben-Gvir Smotrich). Inferred: anti-Netanyahu bloc.

## B5 Palestinian question
- Bennett: wants to annex about 60% of the West Bank (Area C) per IMEU, a critical source [8]; "not a centimeter" line at launch [8]. Lapid long supports separation / Palestinian state but also "maximum Jews on maximum land" [8]. The joint list's position is unclear. Inferred: against statehood.

## B6 Security and alliances
- National security plan page exists (not read) [1]; regional alliances [2].

## B7 Religion and state
- Bennett backs Shabbat public transport and civil marriage (20 Apr 2026); religion-and-state plan page exists [1][2].

## B8 October 7 accountability
- State commission of inquiry, first government action [1][2]. Page /vaada/ "setting up a state commission" [1].

## B9 Diaspora / hasbara spending
- Plan pages for Diaspora Jewry and aliyah exist (not read) [1]. No spending position found.

## How the party presents itself
Two-leader merger defined first by "replace Netanyahu" and a "servers first" (Meshartim) ethos, but with an unusually detailed set of web plan pages and numeric targets, and a rental housing plan. Programme-led with a strong anti-X component.
