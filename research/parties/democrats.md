# The Democrats (הדמוקרטים בראשות יאיר גולן, ballot letters אמת)
Leader: Yair Golan | Components: Labor (The Democrats) + Meretz | Polled seats (10–22 Oct mean): 9.0 | Researched: 2026-10-27 by Claude Sonnet 5.5

## Platform availability (A6)
- Published: **yes, full**. Hebrew PDF, 16 pages, August 2026: https://democrats.org.il/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/plan-8-26-he.pdf (downloaded and read, confirmed) [1]. Topic pages at yes.democrats.org.il/topics/* (security, democracy, economy-society, religion-state, crime, education, environment, LGBTQ+) [2]. Site in English, Arabic, Russian, Spanish; the PDF itself was only found in Hebrew (confirmed for Hebrew only) [2].
- Costed: no costing seen. Consultative mechanism: none found. Accountability: PM 8-year term limit, no indicted ministers/PM, tougher penalties for corruption [1].

## A1 Tenant protection
- The PDF names "שכר דירה שמנפץ חלומות" ("rent that shatters dreams") as a burden on young families, but its only remedy is "נרחיב משמעותית את האפשרויות לשכירות ארוכת טווח ולדיור ציבורי בר-השגה" (significantly expand long-term rental and affordable public housing) [1]. Confirmed. No eviction, tenancy-at-will, agent-fee or enforcement measures in the PDF (searched the full text for שכירות, תיווך, פינוי, שוכר, דייר).
- Record (Meretz side): Tamar Zandberg (Meretz) co-sponsored the 2017 Fair Rental Law (landlord pays agent fee, deposit cap, repair duty, 90/60-day notice; rent-rise clause removed) [3][4]; Stav Shaffir (then Zionist Union/Labor) co-led it with Folkman [3]. Dov Khenin (Joint List) also. Confirmed for 2017.
- No 25th-Knesset Democrats/Labor/Meretz tenant bill found (searched: Lazimi; Zandberg; Hebrew "הצעת חוק שכירות הוגנת"). Naama Lazimi (No. 2) is described as having led social struggles in housing and workers' rights (Hamichlol; not verified further). Possible bills exist on the Knesset database but were not located; do not infer absence.

## A2 Housing affordability
- PDF point 4 "נטפל במשבר הדיור מהשורש": dense urban planning and urban renewal near transport/employment, varied unit sizes, expanded long-term rental and affordable public housing [1]. Confirmed. No numbers. Wikipedia: backs affordable housing, welfare state [5].

## A3 Cost of living and competition
- PDF point 3 "נוריד את יוקר המחיה": break concentration, fight food, drug and energy cartels, lower public transport fares, widen price-controlled goods, cut health/education out-of-pocket costs [1]. Confirmed. "Expand price controls" runs against Daniel's pro-competition anchor; no import liberalisation found.
- Also: shorter work week, more leave, expanded public early-childhood framework [1].

## A4 Working state
- Education, health, welfare rebuild, public transport, north and south rehabilitation, digital access to justice, more judges and appeals courts [1]. Good breadth, thin on bureaucracy specifics.

## A5 Global reputation
- Israel "perceived in the region as extreme", isolated; leads a regional initiative with the US and moderate Arab states; Saudi normalisation, IMEC [1]. Substantive conduct-and-policy framing. Confirmed.

## B1 Haredi enlistment
- "שוויון מוחלט בנטל": equal national service for all citizens; stop funding Haredi education networks; make state-Haredi schooling the main track [1]. Not led with.

## B2 Judicial overhaul
- Repeal all "regime coup" laws, protect court independence, repeal judges' committee law and religious courts expansion law [1].

## B3 Netanyahu
- Platform blames Netanyahu governments for policy of reaction; strong anti-corruption and "no indicted PM" [1]. Firmly opposed (inferred from platform and bloc).

## B4 Far right in government
- Attacks "the most extreme and religious government in Israel's history", annexation and sectoral budgets [1].

## B5 Palestinian question
- Two-state solution; halt annexation; PA role in Gaza day after; "responsible diplomatic move" with Palestinians [1][5].

## B6 Security and alliances
- Strong military plus regional initiative; Iran isolation; strengthen security coordination with PA; Lebanon international force [1].

## B7 Religion and state
- Civil marriage and divorce, end Rabbinate monopoly on conversion and kashrut, public transport on Shabbat, fight religious coercion [1]. Strongest on this of my four lists.

## B8 October 7 accountability
- State commission of inquiry, implement conclusions in full [1].

## B9 Diaspora / hasbara spending
- No evidence found (searched PDF text for תפוצות, הסברה, דיפלומט).

## How the party presents itself
Programme-led: a long, structured platform in "five steps per theme" format with priorities stated plainly; though also framed against the "extremist government". Closest of my four lists to the meta guide on documentation.
