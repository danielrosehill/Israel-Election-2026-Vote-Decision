# Likud (הליכוד בהנהגת בנימין נתניהו לראשות הממשלה, ballot letters מחל)
Leader: Benjamin Netanyahu | Components: Likud; New Hope – The National Right | Polled seats (10–22 Oct mean): 23.4 | Researched: 2026-10-27 by Claude Sonnet 5.5 (second pass: WebSearch EN+HE, WebFetch, curl)

## Platform availability (A6)
- No platform document. The IDI 2026 elections page links Likud's site section "values" (https://www.likud.org.il/?lang=en#values) as its platform [1]. WebFetch of that page (confirmed): six principles ("ששת העמודים"): security; free economy (free market, less regulation, more competition); Jerusalem/Land of Israel; education/identity; society and periphery (investment in periphery, housing, health, infrastructure); Israel in the world (alliances, peace circle) [2]. No policy specifics, no costing, no consultative mechanism, no electoral reform. Site navigation in Hebrew, English, French, Russian, Arabic [2]. Hebrew search found no separate 2026 מצע [3]; ynet noted in 2019 Likud presented no housing plan [3] (old). Routes: likud.org.il and IDI page fetched OK; zionutdatit.org.il fetched OK (curl 200); shas.org.il connection refused (WebFetch ECONNREFUSED, curl 000); otzma-yehudit.co.il / otzma.org.il / hatzionut.org.il DNS failures (domains guessed, not confirmed as real); Kan article 403. Agent-browser (browser-personal) route NOT tried. No UTJ site tried.

## A1 Tenant protection
- Searched EN + HE ("חוק שכירות הוגנת", rent, tenant protection, 2026): no Likud policy or government bill found. Context: rents rose 3.5% y/y in Q1 2026; new-tenant rents rose 6.6% vs 2.6% for renewals (Ynet, June 2026) [4]. Fair Rental Law (2017 amendment) exists; a 2023 private bill to bar contracting out of its rent-limit chapter is of unknown status [5]. Haim Katz (Likud) has been acting Construction & Housing minister since June 2025 [6]; no tenant measure found in his tenure (searched, not exhaustive). Rent is not mentioned on Likud's site [2].
## A2 Housing affordability
- Site lists housing only under "society and periphery" with no specifics; presents apartment prices down 1.2% y/y (June–July 2026 transactions) as an achievement [2] (confirmed on the page; index caveated by the page itself). Ministers: Goldknopf (UTJ) Dec 2022–Jun 2025, then Katz (Likud) acting [6]. Smotrich/Goldknopf dispute over the discounted-housing programme (Dira BeHanacha; legal authority lapsed end-2024; Smotrich demanded 50% for reservists) [7]. Average home price about EUR 699k Q2 2026 [8].
## A3 Cost of living and competition
- Site claims: 2024 electricity reform (switching suppliers without smart meter), food prices -0.5% m/m Aug 2026, inflation 1.5% y/y, rate cut to 3.25% Sep 2026 [2] (self-reported). Likud MKs helped defeat Smotrich's personal-import VAT-exemption raise (59–25; Likud rebels) [9][10]. Likud minister Karhi: Smotrich's ministry "out of touch and elitist" [11]. Netanyahu promised in 2022 to cut the cost of living; Israel then OECD's most expensive [12].
## A4 Working state
- Health: Haim Katz (Likud) minister since July 2025 after Buso (Shas) resigned [6]; no urgent-care policy found (EN search). Interior: Levin (Likud) since July 2025 [6]. State Comptroller (Sep 2025): no comprehensive government body managed evacuation in the June 2025 Iran war; compensation inconsistent [13]; Jan 2026: 3.2m residents (33.6%) without standard protected space [14]. Electricity reform [2].
## A5 Global reputation
- Site: "Israel in the world: expanding the circle of peace, strategic alliances" [2]. 2026 budget: public diplomacy about $730m (4x), Foreign Ministry unit under Sa'ar [15]. Netanyahu subject to ICC warrant [15]. Approach is PR spend, not policy change (confirmed spend; characterisation inferred).
## B1 Haredi enlistment
- Likud's Boaz Bismuth sponsored the draft bill: full-time yeshiva students keep exemption, more conscription measures; most Likud MKs expected to back it; Edelstein and Illouz opposed (ToI, 21 Jan 2026) [16]. March 2026 budget passed after NIS 800m to Haredi institutions and a promise of an effective-exemption law [17]. Site cites 4,298 Haredi recruits in 2025–26 vs 2,811 prior year, with a caveat that this is not resolution [2].
## B2 Judicial overhaul
- Coalition passed the Judicial Selection Committee law 27 Mar 2025 (opposition boycott; takes effect after next Knesset) [18]; a second Judicial Selection Law later passed, petitioned by ACRI [19]; Supreme Court panel of 11 to hear from about June 2026 [19]. Likud MKs voted as coalition (inferred from coalition-passed). Levin as Justice Minister architect (background, not sourced here).
## B3 Netanyahu
- Netanyahu is the list's PM candidate (confirmed, ballot name).
## B4 Far right in government
- Likud-led government included Smotrich and Ben-Gvir; Netanyahu urged Otzma and RZP to reunite; Ben-Gvir refused [20].
## B5 Palestinian question
- Site: "strengthening settlement and sovereignty across the country" [2]; no state position beyond that.
## B6 Security and alliances
- Site: security without compromise; claims record $19.2bn defence contracts 2025, Iron Beam delivered Dec 2025, all hostages returned by Jan 2026 [2] (self-reported).
## B7 Religion and state
- Coalition concessions to Haredi parties (budget, draft) [16][17]; no Likud religion-and-state text found.
## B8 October 7 accountability
- Netanyahu has not endorsed a state commission; coalition backed Kallner bill for politically appointed commission; High Court heard petitions Apr 2026, government lawyer: court cannot compel [21][22].
## B9 Diaspora / hasbara spending
- Chikli (Likud) is Diaspora Affairs minister [6]; $730m public diplomacy in 2026 budget with American Jewish groups [15].

## How the party presents itself
Leader-centred ("led by Netanyahu for prime minister"). Programme is six generic principles plus a list of self-reported achievements; no costed policy PDF. Inferred: reason to exist is Netanyahu's continued premiership.
