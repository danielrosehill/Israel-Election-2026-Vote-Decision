# Yisrael Beiteinu (ישראל ביתנו בראשות אביגדור ליברמן, ballot letter ל)
Leader: Avigdor Lieberman | Components: Yisrael Beiteinu only | Polled seats (10–22 Oct mean): 7.2 | Researched: 2026-10-27 by Claude Sonnet 5.5

## Platform availability (A6)
- Published: **yes, web page, no PDF seen**: https://www.beytenu.org.il/party-platform/ ("המצע שלנו"); Hebrew only seen [1]. Page metadata: first published 14 Aug 2024, last modified 23 Oct 2026; contains two overlapping versions with differing wording (incl. refs to the "38th government"), so some content may predate 2026 [1].
- Costed: no (Walla estimated the January economic plan at NIS 5–10bn; the party's figures unseen) [2]. Consultative: none found. Accountability: constitution, constitutional court, two-term PM limit, fewer ministers [1].

## A1 Tenant protection
- No tenant-protection policy found (eviction, tenancy at will, agent fees). Platform rent items are supply-side only: 20,000 long-term rental units a year; rent-to-buy track with up to 30% of rent saved toward a down payment [1]. Confirmed.
- MK Yulia Malinovsky bill: one income-tax credit point (about NIS 2,550/yr) for working renters who own no home (Walla; date unknown, not fetched) [3]. Older: Faina Kirshenbaum 2012 bill tying "affordable housing" to military service (Ynet) [3].
- January 2026 economic plan does not address rent [2].

## A2 Housing affordability
- Housing crisis framed as supply problem; change land tenders so developers compete on build quality and speed, not land price; rent-to-own for young couples [1].
- Jan 2026 plan (Walla, 8 Jan 2026): 90% mortgages up to 40 years for young couples on homes under NIS 2.5m; preferential mortgage and daycare for reservists; NIS 2,500/month tax credits for couples where one partner serves [2]. Confirmed. Service-linked.

## A3 Cost of living and competition
- Platform: "package deal" for economy, less regulation, break monopolies incl. food, end exclusive importers, privatisation [1]. Jan 2026 plan: cost-of-living focus; food price rises cited; funding by cutting Haredi subsidies and child allowances [2]. Strong pro-competition language; also proposes ending child allowances from the fifth child [1]. Maariv commentary criticises unfunded promises [4].

## A4 Working state
- Constitution, fewer ministers, defence budget NIS 70bn to 95bn [1]. No service-delivery or bureaucracy-specific plan found.

## A5 Global reputation
- No evidence found for reputation repair (searched platform page, news). Security-first: "the era of the tie is over" [1].

## B1 Haredi enlistment
- Mandatory draft for every 18-year-old, military or civilian; evaders lose allowances, housing and arnona discounts, public-sector eligibility [1]. Lieberman makes universal conscription a condition for joining a government; blocks state funding for evaders [5]. Leads with it, in the "same story" sense.

## B2 Judicial overhaul
- Opposes the "French law" and the override clause; proposes constitutional court; 90-MK majority needed to topple a government in its first two years [1].

## B3 Netanyahu
- Vocal critic; Dan Illouz left Likud to join the list. Lieberman rejected joining a minority government with Yashar per Wikipedia summary of Yashar (so not a simple "any bloc") [5][6].

## B4 Far right in government
- Not found as a direct statement. Inferred from opposition to Haredi parties and Netanyahu coalitions.

## B5 Palestinian question
- Regional "package deal" rather than a Palestinian-only deal; Jerusalem not negotiable; freedom of action in Gaza, transfer to an agreed international body [1]. "Lieberman Plan" to swap Arab-populated areas (Wikipedia, older) [7]. Death penalty for terrorists, home demolitions [7].

## B6 Security and alliances
- Six security principles, "zero tolerance", more armour, defence budget increase [1].

## B7 Religion and state
- Full separation: civil marriage and divorce, end religious councils, Shabbat public transport by local authority, core studies for state funding; return rabbinical courts to the Justice Ministry. Number 2 Oded Forer: government free of religious influence [1][5].

## B8 October 7 accountability
- Promises a state commission of inquiry, per one outlet [5].

## B9 Diaspora / hasbara spending
- No evidence found.

## How the party presents itself
A long-standing, leader-defined secular-right party with a programme page of concrete proposals (constitution, draft, separation of religion and state). The campaign leads with conscription and removing religious influence from government, i.e. partly defined against the Haredi parties.
