## A1 Tenant protection
- No UTJ policy (EN + HE searched). Goldknopf's Housing tenure Dec 2022–Jun 2025 produced no tenant legislation found [3]. Rents rose 3.5% y/y, new tenants 6.6% [4].
## A2 Housing affordability
- Goldknopf (Agudah) Housing Minister Dec 2022–Jun 2025 [3]: pre-office said he did not know whether there was a housing crisis [5]; repurposed Beit Shemesh projects meant for the general public to Haredim, then apologised; Chikli criticised discriminatory land allocations [5]; opposed Smotrich's bid to give 50% of the discounted-housing programme to reservists, warning a halt would hurt the market [6]. Eichler (UTJ) briefly appointed housing minister in a later reshuffle; Katz (Likud) acting from Jun 2025 [3][5].
## A3 Cost of living and competition

## B1 Haredi enlistment
- Asher: UTJ will not join a government that does not pass the yeshiva-students law; no bloc allegiance [8]. Degel and Agudah agreed: any full-time yeshiva student exempt, no personal sanctions [8][9]. In Jan 2026 UTJ split: Degel (4) backed the Bismuth bill, Agudah (3) opposed any sanctions [10]. UTJ left the coalition in 2025 (July; Housing/others changed) [3][11]. Asher alleged draft-evader arrests aim to disrupt the election [8].
## B2 Judicial overhaul
- Coalition passed Judicial Selection law 27 Mar 2025 [12]; UTJ's position/vote not seen (UTJ had left coalition July 2025, after this vote; inferred supporter of coalition at that date).
