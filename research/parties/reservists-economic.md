# The Reservists and the Economic Party (המילואימניקים והכלכלית בראשות יועז הנדל וירון זליכה, ballot letters די)
Leader: Yoaz Hendel (with Yaron Zelekha, New Economic Party) | Components: The Reservists; New Economic Party | Polled seats (10–22 Oct mean): 2.0 | Researched: 2026-10-27 by Claude Sonnet 5.5

## Platform availability (A6)
- **Confirmed:** web "principles" page https://themiluimnikim.org.il/platform/ [1]. Hebrew only; no PDF; not costed; no consultative or electoral-reform mechanism, except a term limit of two terms/8 years for PM and a "Zionist government" principle. Page covers Reservists content; Zelekha's economic content not separately seen. (JPost tags also show a "Zionist Home–The Reservists" label with Tropper; unresolved, not used.)

## A1 Tenant protection
- No evidence found (searched: platform page: no rent/tenant policy).
## A2 Housing affordability
- Confirmed: no general housing policy; only perks for those who serve (rent/mortgage/arnona discounts, land allocation in priority areas) [1].
## A3 Cost of living and competition
- Confirmed: free economy and competition, fight monopolies to cut cost of living, business-barrier task force, three measurable economic reforms a year, tax breaks for tech employers in Negev/Galilee [1]. Imports not mentioned. JNS: cost of living a twin priority with equal service [2].
## A4 Working state
- Confirmed: regulatory-barrier task force for businesses [1]. Nothing on services/civil defence found.
## A5 Global reputation
- No evidence found (searched: platform page; only regional cooperation against Iranian axis [1]).
## B1 Haredi enlistment
- Confirmed: "חוק גיוס לכולם" at 18; single recruitment authority; non-servers lose vote, tax/arnona discounts, benefits, driving licence; no state funding for non-serving institutions [1]. Hendel: will not sit with Haredi parties [3]. Press: "Serve or lose vote" [3].
## B2 Judicial overhaul
- Confirmed: independent external review of judiciary, split AG role, Basic Law: Legislation, rebalance checks; "under broad agreement" [1]. Pro-reform in part.
## B3 Netanyahu
- Confirmed: Hendel: Netanyahu is not his red line; will recommend whoever brings most Zionist seats; no coalition with Haredim or Arab parties [3]. Platform names no one; PM term limit [1].
## B4 Far right in government
- No evidence found (searched: platform; not named).
## B5 Palestinian question
- Confirmed: opposes Palestinian state; "sovereignty through deeds", C-area, Gaza perimeter control, "voluntary migration directorate" [1].
## B6 Security and alliances
- Confirmed: aggressive offensive doctrine, deterrence, defence industries, dismantle Hezbollah if not disarmed, continue vs Iran [1].
## B7 Religion and state
- Confirmed (weak): service tracks respecting religious lifestyle; no budget without service [1].
## B8 October 7 accountability
- Confirmed: investigate "all failures"; says "state" commission label used as excuse to avoid inquiry [1].
## B9 Diaspora / hasbara spending
- No evidence found (searched: platform page).

## How the party presents itself
Programme-plus-cause: reservist burden-sharing (equal service) and cost of living, in a "Zionist government" without Haredi or Arab parties. Not leader-grievance, but defined partly by anti-Haredi-exemption stance.
