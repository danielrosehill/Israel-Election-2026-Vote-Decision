# Amcha Yisrael (עמך ישראל בראשות עופר וינטר, ballot letters ך)
Leader: Ofer Winter | Components: single list, filed via party "Ami Chai LaAd" | Polled seats (10–22 Oct mean): 1.3 | Researched: 2026-10-27 by Claude Sonnet 5.5

## Platform availability (A6)
- **Confirmed:** "Foundations" (אבני היסוד) web page https://amchaisrael.co.il/foundations [1]. Hebrew with an English version linked. Not a PDF. Uncosted; page says detailed costed work plans will be published separately (not seen).

## A1 Tenant protection
- No evidence found (searched: foundations page).
## A2 Housing affordability
- Confirmed: expand urban renewal, increase supply, strengthen infrastructure; no detail [1].
## A3 Cost of living and competition
- Confirmed: break monopolies, open markets, remove import barriers, cut regulation, "שוק חופשי" [1].
## A4 Working state
- Confirmed: remove bureaucratic obstacles for small/medium business; national anti-crime body (extortion, illegal weapons); national energy/water/transport/computing infrastructure [1].
## A5 Global reputation
- Confirmed (weak): proactive public diplomacy, national public-diplomacy body [1]. Hasbara-style approach.
## B1 Haredi enlistment
- Confirmed: universal service law, Torah-excellence tracks with quotas by objective assessment [1]. Winter will demand service but still join a coalition with Haredim; wants a national-service law before government sworn in [2].
## B2 Judicial overhaul
- Confirmed: split AG role into independent prosecutor and government legal adviser; right of appeal on High Court rulings to a supreme appellate body [1].
## B3 Netanyahu
- Confirmed: Winter says right-wing camp leader is Netanyahu and "only him we will recommend" [2][3].
## B4 Far right in government
- Partial: Ben-Gvir asked Winter to reconsider and refused a surplus-vote agreement [2]. Winter's own view of Ben-Gvir not found.
## B5 Palestinian question
- Confirmed: "voluntary emigration" plan for Gaza residents ("הגירה מרצון") [1]; Winter: "one solution in Gaza: migration" [2].
## B6 Security and alliances
- Confirmed: decisive victory rather than managing rounds; end enemy's will and ability to fight [1].
## B7 Religion and state
- Confirmed: Jewish, Zionist, sovereign state where Judaism "invites rather than coerces"; Torah study valued [1].
## B8 October 7 accountability
- Confirmed: independent inquiry "with full state-commission powers" and power to enforce conclusions [1]. Winter criticises 3 years of failure to set one up [2].
## B9 Diaspora / hasbara spending
- Weak: national public-diplomacy body proposed [1]. Diaspora policy not found.

## How the party presents itself
Programme-and-leader hybrid: ex-IDF general with a "generation of sovereignty" ideological platform; right-wing camp party that commits to recommend Netanyahu. Polls near or under the threshold.
