## A4 Working state (civil defence / national security)
- Ben-Gvir was National Security Minister Dec 2022–Jan 2025 and from Mar 2025 (Otzma left government Jan 2025, returned Mar 2025; Katz acting in between) [5]. I found no source tying him or the police to shelter or civil-defence failures in the June 2025 or 2026 Iran wars. Comptroller (Sep 2025) on the June 2025 war: report did not mention his ministry; found no comprehensive government body managing evacuations; police intervention for Ethiopian families blocked from a Jerusalem shelter helped only temporarily [6]. Home Front Command, not his ministry, holds civil-defence planning [7]. His office called a Comptroller Negev report "distorted and falsified" [7]. High Court ruling limited his powers to ban support for the enemy in wartime; he called it "serious moral confusion" [8]. A dedicated search of Ben-Gvir plus shelters (EN + HE) found nothing: so no evidence either way.
